FAQ

Questions importers ask us

How long does shipping from China to Kampala take?

Sea freight usually takes 30–45 days door to door (around 3–4 weeks at sea, then port clearing and 3–5 days by road from Mombasa). Air freight to Entebbe usually takes 5–10 days including clearing. We give you an ETA when we book and keep it updated on your tracking page.

Which documents do I need to clear goods with URA?

Usually the commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or air waybill, and your TIN. Some goods also need a UNBS PVoC certificate of conformity or an import permit (food, drugs, agricultural or regulated goods). Tell us what you're importing and we'll send you the exact checklist.

How do I track my shipment?

Enter any of these in the tracking box at the top of this page: your Bill of Lading (B/L) number for sea freight, your Air Waybill (AWB) number for air freight, any of your container numbers, or your Laiken tracking number (it starts with LKL-). You'll see the current stage, location, ETA and every update from our team.

Mombasa or Dar es Salaam, which port is better?

It depends on your cargo, the shipping line schedule and where in Uganda it's going. We have agents at both ports and will recommend the faster or cheaper route for each shipment.

Do you handle cargo that's only for clearing?

Yes. If your goods are already shipped, send us the BL or AWB and we'll handle customs clearing, release and delivery.
